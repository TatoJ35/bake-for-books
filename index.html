Our Story

We believe that education is a fundamental right that should belong to everyone, no matter where they live or which gender they are. Every young person deserves the chance to learn, grow, and shape their own future. By raising awareness and funds for this cause, we want to do our part to empower youth, break down barriers, and open doors to education where they have been closed.

Why it matters

Since September 2021, girls in Afghanistan have been banned from school past sixth grade. We can't change policy — but we can fund the people working around it.
